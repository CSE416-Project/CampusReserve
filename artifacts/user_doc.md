# CampusReserve User Documentation (Draft)

**CampusReserve** is a room-reservation platform built specifically for Stony Brook University (SBU) student organizations. CampusReserve streamlines discovery of bookable spaces, booking requests approval, and inter-club coordination while keeping reservations synchronized with SBU’s official scheduling system, 25Live.

---

## 1. Getting Started & User Roles

CampusReserve enforces role-based access control to ensure student organizations, general students, venue managers, and university administrators have the exact tools and permissions they need.

| Role | Who It Is For | Core Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **General Student** | Any SBU student browsing campus activity. | Create an account with a username and password (optional email verification); view and search booked events, check open spaces, and browse the campus map (read-only; cannot submit bookings). |
| **Club Executive Member** | Verified E-board members of a registered SBU student organization. | Search/filter rooms, view campus map, submit booking requests, join waitlists, track requests, message groups, and post on the collaboration forum. |
| **Venue Host** | Employees managing specific campus buildings or rooms. | Update room details, manage space availability and building closures, and message organizations booking their spaces. |
| **Administrator** | Student Affairs and Undergraduate Student Government (USG) staff. | Review and approve/deny requests (with required reasons), manage closures, view usage analytics, and export bookings to 25Live CSV format. |

### Account Creation & Verification
* **General Student Accounts:** You can register and log in using just a **username and password**—no email is required. You can optionally add and verify an email address if you choose to do so upon creating the account.
* **E-Board Verification:** Before submitting booking requests, go to your profile and input your position under "My organizations" to send a request. Booking permissions are scoped to your active leadership term.
* **Email Verification:** To receive email notifications (powered by Brevo), add an email address to your account and click the confirmation link sent to your inbox. Email alerts are only sent to verified addresses.
* **Mobile Access:** CampusReserve is fully accessible via mobile browsers so you can check statuses, reply to messages, or browse rooms and events on the go.

## 2. Finding & Evaluating Spaces (All Users)

### Searching, Filtering
* **Filter Criteria:** Filter spaces by **Building**, **Space Type**, **Minimum Capacity**, **Event Features** (e.g., A/V equipment, stage, movable seating), and timeframes.
* **Interactive Campus Map:** Access the **Campus map** view to see markers for every bookable space across SBU, helping you book and evaluate building locations.

### Understanding Real-Time Room Statuses
CampusReserve updates room statuses within seconds to prevent belated requests and give accurate visibility:
* **Available:** The space is open and eligible for booking during your selected window.
* **Requested:** Another organization has already submitted a request for this time slot and is awaiting approval.
* **Unbookable / Booked:** The space cannot be reserved (due to an approved event, building closure, maintenance, or restricted access). Select the status badge to view scheduled event details or the reason for closure.

### Room Details
Click any room to open its **Room Detail** page, which includes:
* A photo of the space.
* Full list of included amenities and capacity limits.
* Upcoming booked, waitlisted, events in that space.
* Instructions related to the room.

## 3. Guide for General Students

As a **General Student**, you have read-only access to explore what is happening across campus:
* **Browse Booked Events:** Search upcoming club events by building, date, or space to see where campus activities are taking place.
* **Find Empty Spaces:** Filter and view rooms under **Find a space**
* **Campus Map:** Use the interactive map to locate buildings and spaces across SBU.
* *Note:* General Student accounts cannot submit room reservation requests, join waitlists, or view the collaboration forum. If you become an E-board officer, you will have to create a new account with an SBU email.

## 4. Guide for Club Executive Members

### Leadership Verification
1. Create your account and verify your SBU email address.
2. Register your organization status and wait for admin approval.

### Submitting a Booking Request
Locate an available room and select your desired date(s) and event start/end times. An event title is also required.

### Joining a Waitlist
If a popular space is already booked for your preferred time, you will have the option to join the waitlist and view your position.

### Tracking Your Requests & Notifications
Open the **My requests** page to view every submission made by your organization in one place:
* **Statuses:** Requests marked with *Pending review*, *Approved*, *On waitlist*, *Denied*, or *Cancelled*.
* **Denial Reasons:** If a request is denied, an Administrator's stated reason is displayed directly on the request card.
* **Email Notifications:** Once you complete **Email Verification**, you will receive real-time alerts whenever a request is approved, denied, promoted from a waitlist, or receives a new message.

### Coordinating with Other Clubs & Staff
* **Direct Messaging:** Use the built-in messaging tool under **Messages** page to contact organizations, Venue Hosts and Administrators with any questions.
* **Collaboration Forum:** Visit the **Collaboration Forum** to propose joint events, offer excess supplies, or request shared storage and resources with other SBU clubs.

---

## 5. Guide for Administrators (Student Affairs & USG)

### Verifying Leaders
Navigate to the **Verifications** page to view and approve/reject leadership requests

### Reviewing & Approving Requests
1. Navigate to the **Approvals** page to view incoming booking requests.
2. Select **Approve** or **Deny**. **A reason for denial is mandatory** if a request is rejected.

### Analytics & Reporting
Open the **Analytics** page to monitor website statistics, including:
* High-demand rooms.
* Peak booking days and hours.
* Overall usage trends across the university users.

### 25Live Synchronization & CSV Export
CampusReserve operates alongside SBU's official **25Live** booking system:
* **25Live CSV Export:** Under the **25Live** page, click **Download CSV** from the "Ready to export" panel to download confirmed reservations formatted strictly to **25Live CSV import guidelines** for manual upload into the university system.

## 6. Accessibility & Display Controls

CampusReserve is built to meet **WCAG AA** accessibility standards (full keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, and screen-reader labels) and includes customizable display settings in your user preferences:
* **Color & Contrast Sets:** Switch between multiple color and high-contrast themes tailored for visual impairments.
* **Simplification Layer:** Toggle **Simplified View** to reduce visual clutter and streamline on-screen controls for a focused, low-distraction experience.
* **Screen Reader:** Option to read the text on the screen.
* **General reading and site usage support features**
